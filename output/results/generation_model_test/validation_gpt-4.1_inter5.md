**Conclusion:**  
Most gene-pathway associations are robustly supported by both GeneCards GO terms and peer-reviewed literature. However, Tspan5, Tpcn1, and Map1lc3a lack evidence for involvement in axon guidance and neural connectivity, representing clear gaps in current knowledge or annotation.
